Section 4: `## Publication-Lag Policy`
=====================================================================

This is the load-bearing section. Document per D-08:

Subsection 4.1 `### DXY / EEM / VNM (US-market yfinance tickers)`:
- US cash session closes ~22:00 ICT (UTC+7) during US DST (Mar-Nov), ~23:00 ICT in US winter.
- VN session is already closed for the day (VN close 14:45 ICT).
- First VN trading session that can use that US close is the NEXT VN trading day.
- Since US-close date in the CSV is the US local date, `merge_asof(direction='backward')` on VN30 dates naturally lags by 1 VN trading day — the US close of date D is matched to the VN session on D+1 (or later, across weekends/holidays).
- NO manual date shift is required for DXY/EEM/VNM. The natural backward-merge handles it.

Subsection 4.2 `### TNX (US 10Y yield — US-market)`:
- Same as DXY/EEM: US close → VN next session via backward merge. (TNX is excluded from active feature set but retained in schema.)

Subsection 4.3 `### USD/VND (VND=X — 24h FX)`:
- FX trades 24h globally, continuously quoted during VN session.
- Same-day availability is technically possible.
- Excluded from active feature set per research verdict (correlation near zero). Retained in schema for transparency and future research.
- Phase 44 MUST still use `merge_asof(direction='backward')` if it ever consumes USD/VND, to maintain consistency with DXY/EEM treatment.

Subsection 4.4 `### SBV policy events`:
- SBV announcements are typically intra-session (morning or afternoon press releases).
- Policy signal is available to trade on FROM the next VN session open — not the event-day close.
- Merge contract: shift event dates by +1 business day to build an `effective_date` column, then `merge_asof(direction='backward')` on `effective_date`:

```python

    sbv.sort_values('effective_date'),
    left_on='date',
    right_on='effective_date',
    direction='backward',
)
```

- 90-day decay to `neutral` is applied AFTER the merge, not in the CSV.

- DXY and EEM 20-day rolling z-scores are computed ON the merged DataFrame, NOT on the raw proxy CSV.
- This ensures the rolling window respects VN30 trading days (skips VN holidays the proxy-only rolling would not skip).

Section 6: `## Look-Ahead Traps Ruled Out`
=====================================================================

Enumerate at least 3 look-ahead traps per D-09. Use numbered list format:

1. **Same-day DXY/EEM merge is FORBIDDEN.**
   - `merge_asof(direction='forward')` or `direction='nearest'` on same-date DXY/EEM close would let VN decisions made at 09:00 ICT peek at US close that happens 13 hours later. `direction='backward'` is the only correct choice.

2. **SBV intra-session announcement lag.**
   - If a SBV rate hike announces at 14:00 ICT on date D, a VN30 SELL signal firing on date D at 09:30 ICT MUST NOT use the new `tightening` regime tag for that day's decision — the announcement had not yet occurred. The `effective_date = event_date + 1 BusinessDay` shift (Section 4.4) enforces this.

3. **Weekend / holiday bridging is naturally correct under `merge_asof(backward)`.**
   - If Friday US close is the last proxy row and VN market is open Monday (US closed Monday for Presidents' Day, say), VN Monday's merge picks up Friday's US close — correct, no Monday US close exists yet. Last-known-value carry is the intended behavior. DO NOT forward-fill inside the CSV itself — that would fabricate proxy values on non-US-trading days, corrupting the raw-data contract.

4. **Rolling z-score computed on raw proxy DataFrame is WRONG.**
   - Computing 20d z-score on the proxy CSV directly would produce a z-score indexed by US trading days. Mapping that back to VN30 dates via merge would silently use the NEAREST US date's z-score, which may be FUTURE from the VN date's perspective (if the nearest is tomorrow). Z-score MUST be computed on the merged DataFrame AFTER the backward-merge, so the rolling window respects VN30 date ordering.

5. **SBV `direction='nearest'` is FORBIDDEN.**
   - Using `direction='nearest'` on the SBV merge would match a VN date to a FUTURE SBV event. Only `direction='backward'` on `effective_date` is correct.
